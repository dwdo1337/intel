## 10. Copywriting doctrine

### 10.1 The one test every line must pass

> Does this line make the reader feel they can **stop watching**, or that they
> need to **watch harder**?

If it's the second, delete it. That is the entire brand in one question. "Never
miss another call" fails it. "Close the chats, the calls will find you" passes.
Most crypto copy fails it — which is exactly why this will stand out.

### 10.2 Show the mechanism, not the adjective

The reader has been lied to by twelve scanner bots. Adjectives read as noise;
mechanisms read as proof. Trade the adjective for the thing itself.

| Don't write | Write |
| --- | --- |
| "Advanced noise filtering" | "The same guy posting five times counts once." |
| "Real-time enrichment" | "The card fills in while you're still reading it." |
| "Comprehensive coverage" | "18 servers. 1,295 channels. One window." |
| "Smart money insights" | "Shows the KOLs *still holding*, not the ones who already sold." |
| "Accurate data" | "If it doesn't know, it says `unknown`." |
| "Track tokens across platforms" | "It showed up in a second group, then in a Discord server. You saw all three." |

### 10.3 Specificity is the strategy

Numbers are the cheapest credibility you'll ever buy, and this product has real
ones (§6 of Part One). `205 of 500` beats "most". `50–460 ms` beats "instant".
`1,295 channels` beats "hundreds". **Never round a real number into a vague one.**

### 10.4 Lead with the reader's situation, not the product

The first line of anything is a sentence the reader recognises as *their life*.

- ✅ "You're in twenty rooms. You can read one."
- ✅ "The call you missed was on screen. In a window you weren't looking at."
- ❌ "intel. is a desktop application that aggregates…"

The product enters in line two, as the resolution.

### 10.5 The honesty angle is a weapon — headline it

Every competitor overstates. This product renders `unknown` when it doesn't know,
says "not detectable on this chain", and refuses to show a green tick it didn't
earn. **Make that a headline, not a footnote.** To an audience that has been
burned, it is the most differentiating thing you have.

- "Most scanners guess. This one says 'unknown'."
- "Green means checked. Not 'probably'."
- "It will tell you when it has no idea. On purpose."

### 10.6 Voice mechanics

- **Short declaratives.** Full stops over commas. Fragments are fine.
- **Second person.** "You", not "users" or "traders".
- **Lowercase brand**: `intel.` with the dot. Never "Intel".
- **No exclamation marks. No emoji in headlines.**
- **Contractions on** in marketing copy (this brief is formal; the ads are not).
- **Banned**: revolutionise, game-changing, unlock, supercharge, seamless,
  leverage, empower, next-gen, alpha-generating, "10x your", "built different".

### 10.7 Length discipline

| Surface | Headline | Body |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | ≤ 8 words | ≤ 25 words |
| Feature card | ≤ 6 words | 1 sentence, ≤ 20 words |
| X post | ≤ 12 words | ≤ 200 chars |
| Feature section | ≤ 10 words | ≤ 40 words |
| FAQ answer | — | ≤ 50 words, answer in sentence one |

If a line needs a comma to survive, it's too long.

### 10.8 Hook taxonomy — rotate these

Don't post twelve of the same shape.

1. **Recognition** — "You're in twenty rooms and you can read one."
2. **Confession** — "I closed every chat window three weeks ago. Haven't missed a call."
3. **Reframe** — "You're not slow. You're outnumbered."
4. **Single stat** — "205 of 500 tokens were called by more than one person."
5. **Mechanism reveal** — "'Called 7×' usually means one person and six bots."
6. **Contrarian** — "The feature I wanted wasn't more signal. It was permission to stop looking."
7. **Demo** — a clip, one line of setup, no pitch.
8. **Honesty flex** — "It says 'unknown' more than any scanner you've used. That's the point."
9. **Permission** — "Go scan the trenches yourself. This watches the rooms."
10. **Anti-FOMO** — "This does not tell you a call is good. Nothing can."

### 10.9 Ethical floor

Never promise profit or returns. Never imply a call is good. No fake
testimonials, user counts or PnL. State the Discord ToS caveat plainly — the
honesty is on-brand, and hiding it becomes a support problem.

## 11. The campaign — three phases

### PHASE 1 · PRE-LAUNCH — make the *problem* famous

Barely sell the product. Cheapest material, needed first.

19. **Problem posts ×8** — pure recognition, no product. Text-on-dark. These seed
    the audience and cost nothing.
20. **Teaser clips ×4** — 3–6 s loops. A toast sliding in and filling with data.
    No context, no logo until the final frame. Ambiguity is the point.
21. **Build-in-public cards ×6** — a real bug and its fix, framed as craft. You
    have genuine material: *"The notification never updated for months —
    contextIsolation was off, so the IPC bridge silently died."* Traders and devs
    both respect this, and it is true.
22. **"Coming" announcement ×2** — one image, a date, no feature list.
23. **Countdown set ×3** — T-7 / T-1 / T-0, one layout, one number changing.
24. **Outreach one-pager ×1** — for approaching group owners and KOLs directly.

### PHASE 2 · LAUNCH — one loud day

25. **Launch hero ×3** — the "it's live" image, three distinct concepts.
26. **Launch thread ×1** — 8–10 posts: hook → problem → the toast → cross-source
    → honesty → local-only → how to get it → CTA. One image each.
27. **Launch video storyboard ×1** — 45–60 s. Cold open on chaos (twenty
    windows), cut to one toast, end on a closed laptop.
28. **Demo GIF ×1** — the 15 s hero loop for the top of every channel.
29. **Telegram launch posts ×3** — long / medium / one-liner for different groups.
30. **Landing page launch state ×1** — downloads enabled, CA slot populated.
    *(The page currently ships downloads disabled with a small CA placeholder at
    top and bottom — build the enabled variant as a separate state.)*
31. **Repo/press blurb ×1** — 100 words for GitHub About, directories, reposts.
32. **Reply-bait pack ×5** — short posts built to be quote-tweeted, each with one
    strong, checkable number.

### PHASE 3 · POST-LAUNCH — sustain, prove, teach

33. **Feature deep-dives ×6** — thread + image set per capability. Evergreen.
34. **"How it works" explainers ×3** — the distinct-caller rule, the frozen entry,
    holder-vs-trader. App-accurate diagrams.
35. **Changelog cards ×4** — a reusable template for shipping visibly.
36. **Use-case vignettes ×4** — the guy in 20 rooms · the one who sleeps · the one
    who trades another chain · the one who only wants their own picks.
37. **Objection-handling posts ×6** — one per FAQ item.
38. **Comparison posts ×2** — vs. sitting in chats, vs. generic bots. Honest about
    what it does *not* do.
39. **Retention loop ×3** — aimed at existing users: watchlist re-alerts, caller
    track record, chain muting. Features people miss.
40. **UGC template ×1** — a frame users drop their own (anonymised) toast into.

## 13. Package structure

**50 numbered deliverables.** Organise output as:

```
design/promo/
  01-core/        heroes, product shots, feature sections
  02-social/      X, TG, IG, profile kit
  03-motion/      storyboards, shot lists, GIF specs
  04-longform/    landing, one-pager, FAQ, comparison
  05-brand/       kit, icons
  06-prelaunch/
  07-launch/
  08-postlaunch/
  COPY-BANK.md
```

**`COPY-BANK.md` is a required deliverable**, not an afterthought: every headline
and body line you wrote, grouped by phase and surface, so copy can be reused
without opening ten HTML files.

**Do the phases in order.** Phase 1 is needed first and is cheapest (mostly
type). Do not start with the 60-second video.
